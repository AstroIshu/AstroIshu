## `what I build`

### Intelligent Systems

AI/ML systems around language, reasoning, retrieval, prediction, and human interaction, from experimental ML models to LLM applications and agents.

### Psychometrics & Human Behaviour

I explore how **human behaviour can be represented and measured computationally**.

My work touches personality modelling, Big Five and MBTI, emotional intelligence, compatibility, behavioural patterns, and game-based psychometric signals.

### AI Safety & Evaluation

I'm interested in **testing AI systems, not just building them**, including jailbreaks, prompt injection, hallucinations, information leakage, instruction following, adversarial behaviour, and safety benchmarks.

### AI Governance

Researching how increasingly capable AI systems should be **evaluated, regulated, and governed**, with interests in frontier AI, AI policy, regulatory sandboxes, and AI auditing.

### Autonomous Systems

Curious about AI that operates beyond the screen, including robotics, UAVs, computer vision, Edge AI, TinyML, autonomous navigation, multi-agent systems, and defence technology.
