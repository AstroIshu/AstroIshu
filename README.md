## `what I build`

### 🧠 Intelligent Systems

AI/ML systems around language, reasoning, retrieval, prediction and human interaction, everything from experimental ML models to LLM applications and agents.

### 🧬 Psychometry & Human Behaviour

I explore how **human behaviour can be represented and measured computationally**.

My work touches personality modelling, Big Five / MBTI, emotional intelligence, compatibility, behavioural patterns and game-based psychometric signals.

### 🛡️ AI Safety & Evaluation

I'm interested in **testing AI systems, not just building them**: jailbreaks, prompt injection, hallucination, information leakage, instruction following, adversarial behaviour and safety benchmarks.

### ⚖️ AI Governance

Researching how increasingly capable AI systems should be **evaluated, regulated and governed**, with interests in frontier AI, AI policy, regulatory sandboxes and AI auditing.

### 🤖 Autonomous Systems

Curious about AI that operates beyond the screen: robotics, UAVs, computer vision, Edge AI, TinyML, autonomous navigation, multi-agent systems and defence technology.

### 🔬 Experiments

Small ideas, strange hypotheses and things I build simply to answer:

> **“What happens if I try this?”**
